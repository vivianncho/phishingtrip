# Hook artwork source

The four SVG layers were exported from the user-provided Figma design, using
the connected Figma integration. The reference fish is node `2:3` in
`https://www.figma.com/design/djZpvHxg0AuoCooHToIcVL/Untitled?node-id=2-3`.

The fins, body, face, and lantern are assembled at their original relative
coordinates. Animation and verdict-state treatments are added in the mockup.
The reference's product names and instructional copy are not imported.
