---
name: Hotel contact scope
description: Evidence interpretation for hotel main lines versus direct front-desk contacts.
---

A hotel's official overview page labeling a number only as “Tel” supports a
property main telephone contact, not a confirmed direct front-desk service.
Do not infer department routing from a local number or from brand membership.

**Why:** Official Marriott property pages reviewed on 2026-11-14 published
addresses and local telephone links, but did not explicitly identify those
numbers as direct front-desk lines. Calling them front-desk contacts would
overstate the source even though the property identity and number were exact.

**How to apply:** For future directory expansion, read the property's official
page, record its exact identity/location and published service scope, and
require explicit department evidence before using a direct front-desk label.
Missing department evidence means uncertainty, not fraud.
